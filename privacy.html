Easy Arrow Puzzle · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

Easy Arrow Puzzle ("the app") is a puzzle game made by MD SHAMS ALAM SHIKOT. If you have any questions about this policy, email easyarrowpuzzle.help@gmail.com.

Information the app stores itself

The app saves your game progress (current level, stars, and settings such as sound and vibration) only on your device. We do not have accounts, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • How you interact with ads (views, clicks)

This information is collected by Google, not by us. You can learn how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded ads

Some features (an extra heart, extra hints) can be unlocked by choosing to watch a video ad. Watching these ads is always optional.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Game progress never leaves your device. Ad data is handled by Google using its own security practices.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
